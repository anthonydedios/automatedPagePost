Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy explains how the "Contact Closet Poster" application (the "App") uses information when connecting to Facebook on behalf of the Contact Closet De Emilia Facebook Page.

What the App Does

This App is used solely to automatically publish pre-written posts (text and images related to clothing products) to the Contact Closet De Emilia Facebook Page on a scheduled basis. The App does not collect, process, or store any personal information from Facebook users, Page followers, or visitors.

Information We Access

The App uses a Facebook Page Access Token, issued by Facebook, solely to publish content to the Page. This token is stored securely as an encrypted secret within GitHub Actions and is never shared with any third party. The App does not access, read, or store any data belonging to Page followers, commenters, or any other Facebook users.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties. No user data is collected by this App beyond the credentials required to post content to our own Page.

Data Retention

The only data retained is the content queue (captions and image links) used to generate posts, which is stored in a private GitHub repository controlled by the Page owner.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through the Contact Closet De Emilia Facebook Page: https://www.facebook.com/ContactClosetDeEmilia
