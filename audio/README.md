# Audio folder

Drop royalty-free music files here (.mp3, .aac, .m4a, or .wav) and the
reel generator will automatically pick one at random, loop/trim it to
match the video length, and fade it out in the last second.

If this folder is empty, reels are generated silently - that's fine too.

## Where to get music you're actually allowed to use

- **Meta's own Sound Collection** - free, built for exactly this, and
  the safest choice since it's licensed specifically for Facebook/
  Instagram content. Download tracks from Meta Business Suite's Audio
  library (or the Reels composer in the Facebook app) and drop the
  downloaded file in here.
- **YouTube Audio Library** (studio.youtube.com -> Audio Library) - free
  tracks explicitly cleared for reuse; check each track's specific
  license (some require attribution).
- Any music you've personally purchased/licensed with rights that cover
  commercial social media use.

Avoid pulling music from random "free mp3 download" sites or ripping
audio from other videos/songs you don't hold rights to - Facebook's
copyright detection (Rights Manager) actively scans Reels audio, and a
match can get the Reel muted, taken down, or in repeated cases affect
your Page's standing.
